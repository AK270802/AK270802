Baseel Partners — AI Lead Engineer

2024 → present · Cork / London / remote

I own the enterprise AI SaaS platform: six modules — GrowthOS, AuthorityAI, IntentPulse, ConversionAI, CampaignPilot and InsightFabric — covering marketing and growth intelligence for enterprise clients. I set the architecture, make the build-vs-buy calls, and lead the engineers delivering it across the UK, India, UAE and KSA.

The platform runs agentic multi-LLM pipelines for content and campaign automation, XGBoost and Prophet models for intent and demand forecasting, a Feast feature store shared across modules, MLflow for model lifecycle, and Airflow orchestration — all deployed on AWS with Kubernetes.

  • ScopeSix-module platform architecture, end to end — data ingestion to agent orchestration to customer-facing SaaS
  • Team8–10 engineers — ML, backend, data — across distributed delivery teams
  • StackDjango · Celery · Airflow · Feast · MLflow · XGBoost · Prophet · multi-agent LLM infra · AWS · Kubernetes
  • OutcomeA single shared feature store and model lifecycle across all six modules — one platform delivered to four markets instead of four divergent codebases

Cypra.ai — Freelance AI Compliance Engineer

Contract · Saudi-based AI compliance firm

Product architecture for a multi-tool AI compliance ecosystem serving KSA-regulated clients. I defined how the tools fit together — shared data model, common auth and audit surface, and where LLM-driven analysis could be trusted versus where deterministic rule engines had to hold the line.

  • ScopeCross-product architecture for a multi-tool compliance ecosystem
  • TeamSolo architect, working with the founder and in-house developers
  • OutcomeA unified product architecture the firm's tools now build against, replacing per-tool ad-hoc designs
